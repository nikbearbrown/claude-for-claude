## Preface

Most professionals who work with documents, notes, spreadsheets, and scattered folders have already learned that Claude can help them think. This book is about the next step: using Claude Cowork to act on those files — to extract, organize, assemble, rename, and package — while keeping the human firmly in charge of what gets touched, what gets changed, and what gets verified.

That second kind of use is different from chat. Cowork has access to your workspace. It can read many files, produce structured outputs, and execute multi-step plans. That is powerful, and it is also the source of new risk. The discipline this book teaches is not prompting; it is supervised delegation: preparing a bounded workspace, writing a task brief that names exclusions and review points, requiring a plan before any file is modified, and documenting what changed and who checked it.

This book is for non-technical professionals — researchers, educators, analysts, project managers, and operations staff — who want to use agentic AI responsibly without learning to code. It is practical, specific to Claude Cowork, and organized around the judgment calls that matter more than any individual prompt.
